Lost Medical Card Guide

Follow these official steps to get a duplicate DGEHS or CGHS card in Delhi.

Your progress
0 of 4 steps completed

Need treatment right now?

  • Go to your attached dispensary or hospital (AMA) and explain the card is lost.
  • Carry photo ID: Aadhaar, employee or pension ID, or passport.
  • For emergencies, bring payslip, pension slip, old card photo, or department ID.
  • Do not delay urgent care while waiting for the duplicate card.

Which card do you have?

DGEHS duplicates are issued by your salary or pension department. There is no single online reprint portal for all beneficiaries.

Step 1

File a police complaint or FIR

Official rules require proof that the card was lost.

  • Visit your nearest police station, or use Delhi Police online lost-article reporting if available in your area.
  • Obtain a copy of the FIR or written police complaint mentioning loss of your DGEHS / Medical Facility Card.
  • If the card was stolen, mention theft explicitly in the FIR.
  • Keep the original complaint receipt safe.
Sample text for police complaint 
Subject: Loss of DGEHS Medical Facility Card

I, [Your Full Name], resident of [Address], employee/pensioner of [Department Name],
hereby report that my DGEHS Medical Facility Card (Card No.: [if known]) was lost on
[Date] at/near [Location].

I request a copy of this complaint for submission to my department for issuance of a
duplicate card.

Contact: [Phone Number]
Signature: _______________
Date: _______________
Step 2

Apply for a duplicate at your department

Apply at the same administrative office from which you draw salary (if working) or pension (if retired).

Apply to the Additional Director, CGHS Headquarters, Delhi.

Application Written request for duplicate card
FIR / complaint copy Mandatory
Fee Rs. 10
Fee IPO of Rs. 50 payable to "P.A.O. CGHS Delhi"
Photographs Two latest family photographs of the beneficiary
Sample application letter 
To,
The [Card Issuing Authority / Head of Department]
[Department Name]
[Office Address]

Subject: Application for issuance of duplicate DGEHS Medical Facility Card

Respected Sir/Madam,

I, [Your Full Name], [Designation], Employee ID / Pension Payment Order No. [Number],
am a beneficiary under the Delhi Government Employees Health Scheme (DGEHS).

My DGEHS Medical Facility Card was lost on [Date]. A copy of the FIR / police
complaint regarding the loss is enclosed.

I request you to kindly issue a duplicate Medical Facility Card. I am enclosing:
1. Copy of FIR / police complaint
2. Two latest family photographs
3. Fee of Rs. 10/-

Thanking you,

[Your Name]
[Employee ID / PPO No.]
[Contact Number]
[Date]

CGHS also offers a "Print your own card" option on the CGHS website if your record is already in their system.

Step 3

Follow up if delayed

Processing usually takes a few days to a couple of weeks. If your department is slow or unresponsive, contact DGEHS headquarters for guidance.

For CGHS card issues in Delhi, contact Additional Director, CGHS Headquarters, with your application, FIR, photographs, and IPO.

Step 4

After you receive the duplicate

  • Verify name, validity, ward entitlement, and dependent details on the new card.
  • Inform your AMA (attached dispensary) so their records are updated.
  • Store a digital photo or scan of the card for future reference.

Quick checklist

  1. File FIR or police complaint
  2. Apply at salary / pension department (or CGHS HQ)
  3. Submit application, FIR, fee, and 2 photos
  4. Department verifies records and issues duplicate
  5. Update AMA dispensary records

Important notes

  • If you are unsure which scheme applies, check payslips, pension documents, or ask your department HR / admin section.
  • Your department remains the issuing authority for DGEHS; DGEHS HQ can guide you if there are delays.
  • Keep copies of every document you submit.

Based on official DGEHS rules. For the latest policy, confirm with your department or DGEHS Delhi.
